# Brisko Billing — Operator Guide

A quick reference for taking orders at the counter. Keep it simple: pick items, take
payment, hand over the receipt.

## Starting a bill

- **New Bill** — open the **Billing** screen to start a fresh order.
- **Dine-in** — for customers eating in. Set the order type to **Dine-in**.
- **Takeaway** — for orders going out. Set the order type to **Takeaway**.

## Building the order

- **Add item** — tap a menu item to add it to the cart. Tap again to add another.
- **Add option** — when an item asks for a choice (size, topping, variant), pick it before
  it goes in the cart. Options show under the item line.
- Adjust quantities or remove a line directly in the cart.

## Money on the bill

- **Discount** — apply a discount to the bill before payment. The total updates.
- **GST** — the GST line is applied automatically from your saved GST rate and shows on the
  bill. You do not calculate it by hand.
- **Payment** — choose how the customer paid: **Cash**, **UPI**, **Card**, or **Other**.
  Enter the amount and confirm. For cash, the change due is shown.
- **Print** — when you complete payment, the **kitchen slip (KOT)** and the **customer
  receipt** print automatically.

## Setting a bill aside

- **Hold bill** — park the current order (for example, the table is not ready to pay).
  Nothing is charged and no receipt prints.
- **Resume bill** — bring a held bill back to finish it.
- **Cancel** — discard an order you no longer need. A cancelled order is recorded as
  cancelled.

## After the sale

- **Refund** — return money on a completed bill when needed. Open the bill and choose
  refund; follow the prompts.
- **Reprint** — reprint the **receipt** or the **KOT** for a completed bill. Reprinting does
  not create a new sale or a new order.

## Customers

- **Customer** — attach a customer's phone number to a bill to keep their history. Typing a
  known number pulls up the existing customer; a new number is saved automatically.

## Behind the counter

- **Inventory** — check and adjust stock. Stock is reduced automatically when items sell.
- **Reports** — see sales totals, item-wise sales, and payment breakdowns for a chosen
  period. View-only; nothing here changes a bill.
- **Settings** — business details, GSTIN, GST rate, feedback URL, and the **Printer**
  (including **Test print**). Usually set once by the owner/manager.

## If printing fails

Finish taking the money first — the sale is saved regardless. Then check the printer
(power, paper, cable) and use **Settings → Printer → Test print**, or **Reprint** the
receipt once the printer is back.
